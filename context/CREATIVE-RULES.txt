CREATIVE RULES — newer user corrections take priority

SETTINGS
• Vertical 9:16 unless requested otherwise.
• Every video generation targets 720p unless the user requests another resolution. If the chosen route cannot do it, state the limitation; do not quietly change settings or call an upscale native 720p.
• New short clips usually run 4–7 seconds. Reference remakes use the source/task duration. Counted reveals default to 6 seconds; drop-and-point reveals to 5 seconds.
• General image preference: Nano Banana Pro; two images at 2K unless a different count is specified. GPT Image 2 is also liked, not a required substitute. Visual-hook still remakes specifically use the saved Nano Banana 2.1 preference, subject to current availability.
• One video generation per request unless the user explicitly requests more. Multi-shot/multi-video scope must be explicit. No automatic quality retry, extra video edit, “free” extra attempt or duplicate after a timeout. Look up the existing job first.
• Historical model names are preferences, not a current capability/price table. Check the available model schema and parameters before submitting.

REALISM
Look like ordinary iPhone footage. Keep natural exposure, believable skin and fabric texture, slight phone softness, lived-in settings and casual framing. Avoid glossy AI glare, beauty smoothing, studio polish, excessive blur and cinematic lighting unless specifically requested.
Natural movement needs a clear action path, plausible weight, grip before lift, contact shadows and gentle acceleration/deceleration. Smooth does not mean stiff, slow or mechanically stabilized. Environmental movement must fit the scene: passing scenery in a moving car, not arbitrary movement in every parked-car shot.
Use normal fans in believable homes for fan-product content; a themed product does not automatically call for fantasy costumes or a film set.

PRODUCT FIDELITY
Preserve real shape, scale, colors, materials, graphics, trim, seams, pockets, ears/hoods, packaging layout and distinctive parts. Use the actual product reference. Check front, back, folded states, close-ups, reflections and reappearances. A beautiful image with the wrong product fails.
Give each reference one clear job: identity, product, environment, composition, motion or finish. A product-only photo must not replace the intended room with its studio background.

TEXT POLICY DEPENDS ON THE WORKFLOW
Default clean generated footage has no unwanted captions, UI, emojis or watermarks. Normal product shots and shot-for-shot replication preserve real product markings unless removal is requested.
The saved Genjutsu workflow is stricter: zero visible lettering anywhere, including product labels/signs, unless the current user grants a specific exception. Preserve the objects and remove lettering naturally. Do not describe such a result as retaining all printed detail.
Text removal means reconstructing the underlying surface, not blurring, cropping, covering it or deleting the object. Inspect the actual output, especially movement and cuts.

AUDIO ALSO DEPENDS ON THE WORKFLOW
Genjutsu keeps source audio by default; mute only when requested. The saved Seedance background/persistent-layer remake workflows default to silence. Showcase B-roll defaults to silent footage for editing. Reveal formats have their own speech/gasp/ambience rules. Explicit task instructions win. Never combine silent and keep-audio clauses.
Listen to the final export before claiming speech continuity or preserved audio. Metadata alone cannot prove those things.

REFERENCE-USE EXCEPTION
For visual-hook STILL reconstruction, analyze the screenshot locally and rebuild its composition in words. Never upload/pass the source screenshot to generation. Actual replacement product/identity references may be used as authorized. This exception does not prohibit source-frame references in cleanup or shot-for-shot video replication.

WORKING STYLE
Inspect accessible media yourself. Do not ask the creator to narrate visible actions or re-upload accessible files. Execute when generation is requested and a supported authorized connection exists; deliver prompts only when that is the request. Ask only for consequential unknowns.
