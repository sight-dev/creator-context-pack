RESEARCH METHOD

For an already-chosen product, start with audience keywords on Facebook or Instagram hashtags, then competitor accounts. A supplied handle may be the immediate starting point. Inspect accessible videos and comments yourself; do not make the user gather and count everything first.

Record each source URL, post date when available, date checked, actual product, sample basis and what could be observed. “Top videos” requires a clear metric; pinned posts and newest posts are not automatically the most viewed. A caption or thumbnail does not prove the video was watched. Say when audio or comments could not be accessed.

Look for recurring audience language, desires, gift occasions, objections, buying questions, hooks, actions, settings, sounds and B-roll. B-roll means supporting product footage such as a hand opening a box or a detail shot. Distinguish exact phrase counts from grouped themes; do not invent counts.

Deliver a clear likely buyer profile (ICP): who cares, why, buying occasion, objections and evidence. Separate viewer, purchaser and recipient. A creator avatar means the person shown on camera; a customer avatar means a buyer type. Do not confuse the two.

Choose creator clothing, grooming, behavior and setting for this product and action. Ordinary, candid presentation usually fits the realism brief. Hands-only may work. Do not infer a buyer's country, age, income or identity from appearance.

Sounds: identify the actual track or exact source audio when possible. “Original audio by [account]” is not necessarily the song name. Do not call historical audio a current trend without a fresh check. Keep popularity, fit and commercial availability separate.

Concepts must connect to saved organic formats or inspected examples. Explain the source pattern, what changes for this product, why the viewer keeps watching and how the product pays off the opening. Proposed adaptations are untested. Do not invent product features, customer stories or store availability.

VARIANT / AUDIENCE DISCOVERY
Use Reddit as the main discovery source for passionate communities, buying/spending reports and unmet product requests. Then check the EXACT product–audience pairing on accessible organic platforms and stores. A familiar audience can still have an under-served pairing. A large subreddit or enthusiastic comments do not prove sales.
Separate passion, purchase intent, reported purchases, gaps and reasons for doubt. State what stays the same in the product and exactly what changes in the design. “Appears under-served in the searches checked” is more accurate than “untapped.” A store listing proves neither success nor saturation.

The owner supplied this research direction; the detailed evidence table and proposed test format are assistant-designed ways to carry it out. Keep observations, owner claims and assistant recommendations labeled separately.
